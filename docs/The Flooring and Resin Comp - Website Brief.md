## About Us
The Flooring and Resin Company
Crafted through generations. Designed for today.
The Flooring and Resin Company is a proud family-run business with over 50 years of experience in the flooring industry. Built on generations of craftsmanship and care, we deliver quality, reliability, and a personal touch to every project — from large-scale commercial installations to bespoke domestic designs.
🏢 From Homes to Hospitals — Flooring That Works
While our roots lie in domestic flooring, we now specialise in commercial flooring solutions across a wide range of sectors. Our services include:
•	Carpet — A full range of contract carpets and carpet tiles, with extensive experience in care homes and hospitality environments.
•	Vinyl — Loose lay LVT and sheet vinyls, including cap and cove installations ideal for hospitals, kitchens, and wet rooms — designed for hygiene and ease of cleaning.
•	Laminate — Engineered and solid wood laminate flooring, offering durability with aesthetic appeal.
•	Resin Flooring — Our most exciting and innovative offering, where creativity meets performance.
🎨 Resin That Redefines Surfaces
We’re especially passionate about resin surfaces, which allow for completely unique, durable finishes — not just for floors, but for any hard surface, including countertops, feature walls and even customised items such as decorative ornamental images, chopping boards, doorsteps etc.  Our creative edge sets us apart: we’ve begun incorporating original artwork and bespoke de-signs into resin installations, transforming functional surfaces into statement pieces.
One of our proudest showcases is Phoenix Passage 2012 by Canadian artist Greg Deda, cur-rently on display at the Chelsea Art Gallery — a stunning example of how art and flooring can merge to inspire and impress.
💫 Why Choose Us?
At The Flooring and Resin Company, we combine technical precision with artistic flair — and a distinctly personal, female-led approach to service. Whether you're a commercial client seek-ing robust solutions or a homeowner dreaming of something extraordinary, we’re here to meet your flooring needs with creativity, care, and confidence.
